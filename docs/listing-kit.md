## Master pitch (use everywhere, editable)

**One-liner (≤120 chars):**
Open-source Apollo competitor — discovery + SMTP verification gate keeps cold email bounce under 4%.

**Short description (≤250 chars):**
Verdict is an open-source cold-outreach engine and Apollo/Hunter/Instantly alternative. It discovers emails from public-web evidence, verifies every mailbox over SMTP before it ships, flags catch-all domains and corporate gateways, and suppresses dead mailboxes — holding bounce rate under 4% instead of the ~9% typical of list-based tools.

**Long description (for SaaSHub + directories):**
Verdict is an open-source cold-outreach engine that refuses to send what will bounce. Email discovery pulls candidates from public-web evidence instead of a paid contact database; an SMTP verification gate probes every mailbox before it ships; catch-all domains (which accept any address) and enterprise gateways (Mimecast/Proofpoint) are classified honestly. Confirmed-dead mailboxes are suppressed forever, so your sender reputation never takes the damage. MIT licensed, self-hostable, your data stays on your servers.
